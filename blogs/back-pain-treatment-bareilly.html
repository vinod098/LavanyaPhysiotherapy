🔍 Common Causes of Back Pain in Bareilly

Understanding the root cause of your back pain is the first step toward effective treatment. Here are the most common causes we see at our Bareilly physiotherapy clinic:

1. Sedentary Lifestyle & Poor Posture

Many Bareilly residents work in offices or spend long hours sitting, leading to muscle imbalances and spinal misalignment.

2. Occupational Hazards

Jobs involving heavy lifting, repetitive movements, or prolonged standing can strain the back muscles and spine.

3. Age-Related Degeneration

As we age, the spine naturally undergoes wear and tear, leading to conditions like arthritis and disc degeneration.

4. Stress and Tension

Mental stress often manifests as physical tension in the back and neck muscles.

5. Previous Injuries

Old sports injuries, accidents, or falls can cause chronic back pain if not properly rehabilitated.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Back Pain Treatment in Bareilly

How long does it take to see results from physiotherapy?

Most patients begin to feel improvement within 2-3 sessions. Significant pain reduction typically occurs within 2-4 weeks of consistent treatment. However, the timeline varies depending on the severity of your condition and your commitment to the treatment plan.

Is physiotherapy painful?

Physiotherapy should not be painful. While you may experience some discomfort during certain techniques, our goal is to reduce your pain, not increase it. We always work within your comfort level and adjust treatments accordingly.

Can I get physiotherapy at home in Bareilly?

Yes! We provide comprehensive home physiotherapy services throughout Bareilly. Our qualified physiotherapist will come to your home with all necessary equipment and provide the same quality treatment you'd receive at our clinic.

What should I wear for my physiotherapy session?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows easy movement. Avoid tight jeans or restrictive clothing. For back pain treatment, we may need access to your back, so consider wearing a t-shirt or tank top.

How many sessions will I need for back pain?

The number of sessions varies based on your specific condition, severity, and response to treatment. Most patients require 6-12 sessions for significant improvement, but we'll create a personalized treatment plan during your initial assessment.
